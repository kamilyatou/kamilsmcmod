# Kamil's Mod (`kamilsmcmod`)

A Fabric Minecraft mod targeting **Minecraft 26.3** with Java 25.

## Planned Features

### 1. The Bad Arms Dealer (Nitwit Retheme)
* Replaces the vanilla Nitwit with a shady, black-suited dealer.
* Unlocks secret trades for Nitwits selling defective and scam items:
  * Over-enchanted weapons with 1 durability remaining (Curse of Vanishing).
  * Crossbows with the *Unreliability* curse.
  * Scam potions and suspicious items.
* Nervous AI behavior around Iron Golems.

### 2. The RE4 Merchant (Wandering Trader Retheme)
* Rethemes the Wandering Trader inspired by the iconic Resident Evil 4 Merchant.
* Features custom voice lines (*"What're ya buyin', stranger?"*, *"Hehehehe, thank you!"*).
* Sells actually useful high-tier combat gear, ammo, and buys gems & relics at a high price.

### 3. Custom Enchantments
* **Unreliability**: A ranged weapon curse causing weapons to jam, misfire, backfire, or drop projectiles at your feet.
